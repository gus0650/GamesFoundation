W E L C O M E !

GAMES FOUNDATION is an organisation that helps independent game developers create amazing digital games and market them to a worldwide audience.

We can assist you when raising money from investors, government programs, crowdfunding campaigns or publishers. We provide consulting, mentoring and other services that allow you to maximize the impact and the success of your game project.

We also help investors find the best projects and investment opportunities in the games and digital media sector. Video games are a growing market with plenty of opportunity, but also rather complex. With our help and expertise, investors can lower their risk and participate in the ongoing growth of this market.


 

S E R V I C E S

Here's what we can do for you.

FOR INVESTORS

help up-and-coming artists make their vision a reality // learn to understand the business of digital games // identify the best investment opportunities // benefit from professional project supervision // maximize your return on investment. 		   

FOR DEVELOPERS

find partners to complement your skillset // legal advice // get our game project funded // setting up your games company // manage your project // develop your idea into a full-fledged game // release your game on all major platforms.
