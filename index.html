W E L C O M E !

GAMES FOUNDATION is a production company that creates digital games for the international market.

You have an amazing game and you need help getting it funded and published?

We can assist you when raising money from investors, government programs, crowdfunding campaigns or publishers. We can put your game out on all major platforms. We work on a pure revenue share model. No upfront cost.


 

S E R V I C E S

Here's what we can do for you.

FOR INVESTORS

You have money and you want to invest into digital games. We show you a porfolio of games and you can choose which ones you like. You co-fund the development of those games. We guarantee that the games will be completed and published. You get a share of the revenue.

Apply here

   

FOR DEVELOPERS

You have a game in development. We help you raise money from investors or government programmes. You take the money and make your game. We get your game published. You get most of the revenue, while we keep a small share of the revenue. Monthly reporting, full transparency, no bullshit. We do all the legal, taxes, and administrative work for you. You don't need to have your own company.

Apply here
